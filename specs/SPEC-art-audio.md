## Objective

Every game asset (pixel art, tiles, UI, VFX, SFX, music) is either generated by Claude Code from scripts checked into the repo, or sourced by Claude Code from a free library under a license that allows it. Neither developer hand-draws, records, or composes anything. Developers review assets from screenshots and approve or request changes. Assets are placeholder-first so systems ship on schedule, and every content item gets a real asset in the week its logic lands, not in week 12.

## Production rules

- **Reproducible.** A PNG under `assets/` is either the output of a script under `tools/art/` (committed together) or a sourced file with a row in `CREDITS.md`. No PNG exists without one of these two origins. `tools/art/build_all.py` regenerates every generated asset; `--check` diffs against the committed PNGs (adding it to CI is a CI change: ask first).
- **Editable.** Generated sprites are described by data (palette index arrays, shape primitives, or small per-frame pixel maps in `.py` or `.json` next to the generator), so a change request is a parameter edit and a re-run, never pixel pushing.
- **Licensed.** Sourced assets are CC0, CC-BY 3.0/4.0, OFL (fonts), or an explicit "free for commercial use" license from Kenney, OpenGameArt, itch.io, Freesound, or similar libraries. CC-BY-SA, CC-BY-NC, GPL-only art, and "free for personal use" are rejected. Every sourced file gets a row in `CREDITS.md` before it is committed. CC-BY attribution text also appears in the in-game credits roll (`ui-hud`, week 10).
- **Lane-owned.** Asset tasks follow module ownership. Pit lane (Dev A reviews): player characters, enemies, bosses, weapons, mutations, ability VFX and icons, biome tilesets, hazards, combat VFX, combat and enemy SFX. Surface lane (Dev B reviews): UI theme, fonts, HUD and menus, relic and synergy icons, town tiles and buildings, NPC sprites and portraits, dialogue UI, music, UI SFX, `AudioManager`, `CREDITS.md`.
- **Reviewed.** No real (non-placeholder) asset is merged without a review sheet screenshot approved by the owning developer. See "Review step".
- **Never cut.** If a real asset is rejected twice, the placeholder stays and the item goes on the week 12 polish list. Counts in "Asset inventory" are never reduced.

### Outline and shading rules

- 1 px outline in near-black `#1A1420` on every sprite and prop. The outline is on the sprite, never a shader, except the elite outline (`enemies-ai`) and selection highlight, which are shaders.
- Light from top-left (screen space). Top faces highlight, right and bottom faces shadow. Iso tiles: top face base color, left face dark step, right face shadow step.
- 3 to 4 shade steps per material, taken from the ramp, no in-between blends. Checker dither only on tiles and bosses, never on 32x32 or 48x48 characters.
- Selective outline: inner lines use the dark ramp step, not black.
- No anti-aliasing; sprites are drawn in indexed color and exported RGBA with a transparent background.
- Ground shadow: a separate 1-color ellipse sprite (`assets/sprites/common/shadow_<size>.png`), tinted by the biome `CanvasModulate`, not baked into character frames.
- Corrupted enemy variants (The Heart) are the base sheet plus the `corrupted.gdshader` palette swap toward the_heart ramp; no second sheet.
- Readability rule: every enemy has a silhouette distinguishable from every other enemy in its biome at 1x, and its telegraph (`windup`) frame changes silhouette, not just color.

### Naming

Categories: `players`, `enemies`, `bosses`, `npcs`, `weapons`, `projectiles`, `pickups`, `props`, `common`. Tiles under `assets/tilesets/<biome>/`. Icons under `assets/icons/<category>/<id>.png`. ## Animation

4 drawn directions, mirrored to 8 at runtime. Rows drawn: `s`, `e`, `n`, `ne`. Mirrors: `w` from `e`, `nw` from `ne`, `sw` and `se` share one drawn row (`se`) mirrored. A sprite that is asymmetric in a way that matters for gameplay (shield arm, cannon side) draws the extra row and the manifest says so. The manifest lists drawn rows; `make_sprite_frames.gd` emits the mirrored animations by writing `flip_h` into the manifest-derived `SpriteFrames` metadata that `FacingSprite` (player-controller) reads. Design doc section 17 says "8-direction sprite sheets": the result is 8 directions on screen; drawing 4 and mirroring is the production method, recorded here as the human decision of 2026-10-19 (not a cut).

Frame counts (10 fps unless the manifest says otherwise):

| Animation | Frames | Loop | Who has it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `idle` | 4 | yes | everyone |
| `run` | 6 | yes | everyone that moves (`walk` for NPCs) |
| `attack_light` | 4 | no | players (one set per weapon type, 5), melee enemies |
| `attack_heavy` | 6 | no | players (per weapon type), heavy enemies |
| `cast` | 4 | no | players, caster enemies |
| `dodge` | 4 | no | players |
| `hurt` | 2 | no | everyone |
| `downed` | 2 | yes | players, escort NPC |
| `death` | 6 | no | everyone |
| `windup` | 3 | no | enemies and bosses (the readable tell before `attack`) |
| `special_<n>` | 4 to 8 | no | bosses, one per attack listed in `BossPhase` |
| `spawn` | 4 | no | enemies (rise from ground), bosses (intro) |
| `phase_transition` | 8 | no | bosses with 2 or more phases |

Minimum set for every character and enemy: `idle`, `run`, `attack`, `hurt`, `death`. Players get the full list. A character sheet is one PNG, rows = drawn directions, columns = frames in the order above, with the manifest giving column offsets per animation. Weapon-type attack variants are separate sheets `players/<id>_<weapon_type>.png` so the body sheet stays small.

## Sourced assets (found by Claude)

Preferred sources, in order: Kenney (CC0), OpenGameArt filtered to CC0 then CC-BY, itch.io packs with explicit CC0 or "free, commercial OK" terms, Freesound filtered to CC0, Google Fonts (OFL). Categories to source rather than generate: pixel fonts (UI, damage numbers, title), ambient loops, foley SFX that are hard to synthesize (water, fire crackle, stone collapse, crowd), and music where a fitting CC0 track exists.

Workflow per asset: find; record license and URL; download into a fresh scratch folder (untrusted data); convert to project format (PNG RGBA, OGG Vorbis 44.1 kHz for audio); rename to the project id; add a `CREDITS.md` row; commit asset and row together. A sourced sprite that does not match the palette goes through `tools/art/recolor.py` and is still credited with "recolored" in the modifications column.

`CREDITS.md` (repo root) columns: `asset path | source URL | author | license | license URL | modifications`. One row per file, or per pack with a path glob. `tests/integration/test_credits.gd` asserts every file under `assets/` that `build_all.py` does not produce has a row with an allowed license, and that every row's path exists.

## VFX (made by Claude in Godot)

`GPUParticles2D` library under `scenes/vfx/`, one emitter scene per id, textures from `gen_vfx_textures.py`, tints from `palette.ELEMENT`. Ids: `burn`, `chill`, `freeze`, `shock`, `toxin`, `mark`, `bleed`, `unravel` (statuses); one per element combo (6); `hit_<weapon_type>` (5); `hit_heavy`, `crit`, `dodge_trail`, `perfect_dodge`, `pickup_sparkle`, `room_clear`, `level_up`, `elite_spawn`, `boss_death`, `shrine_idle`, `rope_idle`, `portal`; `ability_<id>` (30); `ult_<id>` (character and class ultimates plus alternates); `relic_proc_<tag>` (one per relic tag, so 88 relics share about 20 procs). Shaders under `assets/shaders/`: `hit_flash`, `elite_outline`, `corrupted` (palette swap), `dissolve` (death), `water_distort`, `heat_haze`, `crystal_refract`, `spore_fog`, `vignette_darkness`. `Vfx.spawn(id, pos, tint)` through a pool in `World/FX`; spawned locally on every peer from replicated events; cap 128 live emitters. Each ability, relic proc, and mutation names its VFX id in its data.

## SFX (generated by Claude)

`tools/audio/synth.py`: sfxr-style generator (square, saw, sine, noise; ADSR; pitch slide; vibrato; low-pass; bit-crush) that renders 44.1 kHz mono. Each SFX is a parameter dict in `tools/audio/sfx/<id>.json`; `tools/audio/build_sfx.py` renders every json to `assets/audio/sfx/<id>.ogg` (WAV then OGG via `soundfile` or `ffmpeg`; dev-only, see open questions). Ids that synth cannot do well (water, fire, stone, crowd) are sourced CC0 and credited. Loudness target -16 LUFS short-term per clip, peak -1 dBFS, normalized by the build script. `tools/audio/review_sheet.py` writes a waveform + spectrogram PNG per batch for the review step.

Full SFX list (every id present by week 12, placeholder click before): hit per weapon type (5), heavy hit, crit, dodge, perfect dodge, pickup gold/ore/echo/rune/relic, potion, ability cast per school (9), ultimate, class skill per class (7), enemy hurt small/large, enemy death, elite spawn, boss roar, boss phase, boss death, door lock/unlock, room clear, rope, chest, breakable, shrine pick, anvil, cursed altar, revive, downed, player death, level-up/mastery, UI click/hover/confirm/cancel/error, dialogue blip, menu open/close, hazards (rocks, cart, gas, spore, beam, water, lava, steam), footsteps per biome surface (6 biomes + town, 2 variants each).

`AudioManager` autoload (unchanged API):

```gdscript
func play_music(track: AudioStream, fade_seconds: float = 1.5) -> void   # crossfade on two players
func play_sfx(id: StringName, pos: Vector2 = Vector2.INF, pitch_variance: float = 0.1) -> void   # pooled AudioStreamPlayer2D (positional) or non-positional
func set_bus_volume(bus: StringName, linear: float) -> void
func stop_music(fade_seconds: float) -> void
```

Buses: Master, Music, SFX, UI. SFX ids map to files in `assets/audio/sfx/<id>.ogg` via a `SfxBank` Resource; missing id logs once and plays a placeholder click. Positional SFX for hits, pickups, enemies; UI non-positional. Time effects do not alter pitch.

## Music (sourced or generated by Claude)

Tracks: main menu, town theme, 6 biome tracks, mini-boss theme, 5 biome boss themes, final boss track, victory, death, credits = 17. Two production paths, chosen per track and recorded in `CREDITS.md`: (a) sourced CC0 or CC-BY loops from OpenGameArt, Kenney, or itch.io, trimmed to a seamless loop; (b) generated chiptune via `tools/audio/tracker.py` (pattern-based: two square, one triangle, one noise channel; note events in `tools/audio/music/<id>.json`; rendered to OGG). Targets: loops 60 to 120 s, -14 LUFS integrated, `loop = true` in the `.ogg.import`. Each biome track's json carries a brief (tempo, key, instrumentation) derived from the biome palette mood. Placeholder loop until a track's week.

## Lighting

`CanvasModulate` per biome from the palette Light column, `PointLight2D` on players and torches with a soft 64 px texture from `gen_vfx_textures.py`, enemy glow for elites; capped at 16 lights per room and pooled. Compatibility renderer: no shadows (cost), faked ground shadows (sprite).

## Review step

Every asset PR includes a review sheet, and the owning developer approves it before merge:

1. Claude runs `tools/art/review_sheet.py` on the new assets and writes `docs/asset-review/week-<N>/<lane>-<topic>.png` (1x and 4x, every direction and animation row labeled, placed on the biome floor tile, palette strip at the bottom). Audio: `tools/audio/review_sheet.py` writes a waveform + spectrogram PNG and the reviewer plays the OGGs.
2. Claude runs the game to the relevant room with the debug console (`spawn <id>`, `floor <n>`, `vfx <id>`, `sfx <id>`) and captures an in-engine screenshot with `godot --path . -s tools/art/capture.gd --scene=<scene> --out=<png>` (or the Godot MCP `run_project` plus a viewport screenshot), saved next to the review sheet.
3. The PR description embeds both images and lists asset ids, generator or source, palette used, and `CREDITS.md` rows added.
4. The owning developer replies `approved` or lists changes. Changes are parameter edits and a re-run; the sheet is regenerated. Two rejections send the item to the week 12 polish list with the placeholder kept.
5. Approved sheets stay committed (small PNGs). `docs/asset-review/INDEX.md` lists every sheet with status (`pending`, `approved`, `changes`, `polish-list`).

WEEKLY.md checklist gains one line: "every real asset landed this week has an approved review sheet".
